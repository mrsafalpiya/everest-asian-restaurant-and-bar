# Illustration brief: the chef making naan, anime style

Client request (2026-09-22): an anime-style illustration of the chef making naan, shown on the home page. It sits in the Concept section of both directions, in a slot the artwork must fill.

## Deliverable

- One illustration, landscape, **1680 × 1120 px minimum** (3:2). A 4:3 crop must also work: keep the chef and the naan inside the central 4:3 area.
- Background: transparent PNG preferred (the page supplies the ground per direction), plus one flattened JPG on warm cream for reference.
- No text, no logo inside the artwork.
- Subject: the restaurant's chef (Nepali, trained in India and Dubai; ask the client for a reference photo so the likeness is theirs, not a generic character) at the tandoor, slapping a naan onto the clay wall or lifting a blistered naan out on a skewer. Steam, a little flour dust, warm oven glow from below.
- Style: modern anime / manga cel shading, clean line work, warm palette. Think a Ghibli kitchen scene or a "Shokugeki" cooking beat, not chibi and not photoreal. Friendly expression, sleeves rolled, apron. Avoid stereotype props.
- Palette anchors so it sits in every direction: forest green #0F3D2E / #123527 (apron or wall tile), gold #CE9E49 (oven glow, naan blister highlights), sumi #161616 lines, warm cream skin/ground tones. A single crimson accent is fine.

## Prompt (for an image model)

"Anime-style illustration, cel shaded, clean ink lines. A smiling Nepali chef in a white jacket and dark green apron leans over a glowing clay tandoor, lifting a puffed, blistered naan on a long skewer with one hand while slapping fresh dough with the other. Flour dust, rising steam, warm golden light from the oven on his face. Restaurant kitchen, shallow background, muted forest green tiles and brass pots. Landscape 3:2, subject centered, transparent background, no text."

Negative: text, watermark, logo, chibi, photorealism, extra hands.

## Where it goes

- Direction B (noren): inside the gold hairline frame with the 栃木 seal, 520 × 560 crop on desktop (portrait crop of the same art, so keep the chef upright and central).
- Direction C (broadside): the printed plate at the left of Concept, 560 × 720 on desktop; it is shown as a halftone/duotone print on cream, so strong line work matters.
